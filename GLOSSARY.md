# Glossary

**Talk**:
A presentation published as a card on the landing page, with its own slide deck, conference, year and tags.

**Tag**:
A topic label on a Talk. Tags drive the landing page filter and appear as chips on the card.

**Platform tag**:
A Tag naming a mobile platform (Android or iOS). A Talk with a Platform tag also matches the Mobile filter, even if it does not list Mobile itself.
_Avoid_: OS tag

**Base deck**:
The deck a Talk is first built as, usually in English. It lives at the Talk's own path and is the source every Edition is made from.

**Edition**:
A copy of a Talk's Base deck made for one event, usually in another language, named after the event and year (`ourense26`, `jerez26`, `jaen2026`). It is made from the frozen Base deck, published under the Base deck's path, and never edited on its own.
_Avoid_: Spanish copy, translated copy, version, i18n
