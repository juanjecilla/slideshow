# Glossary

**Talk**:
A presentation published as a card on the landing page, with its own slide deck, conference, year and tags.

**Tag**:
A topic label on a Talk. Tags drive the landing page filter and appear as chips on the card.

**Platform tag**:
A Tag naming a mobile platform (Android or iOS). A Talk with a Platform tag also matches the Mobile filter, even if it does not list Mobile itself.
_Avoid_: OS tag
